This site: a small solar system you navigate. Each section is a planet; choosing one turns the camera to it and opens a terminal window with its contents. The sun is a switch: it stops time, and every orbit with it.

It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no framework and no build step. Each section's entries live in their own HTML file, one article per entry, so adding one is a single edit.

Most of the work went into making it hold up everywhere. Phones get a lighter scene, with the stars drawn once instead of live, because Safari can drop a live canvas under memory pressure. The whole site works from the keyboard, follows reduced-motion and high-contrast settings, stays readable with enlarged text, and still shows the contact details with JavaScript turned off.
