Four rounds of studies for the star at the center of this site: a pulsing glow, an eclipse, shaded spheres, mist particles, eight diffraction stars and five black holes, before settling on a single horizontal lens streak.

Every candidate was drawn live at its real on-screen size next to an orbit, with a switch to compare its "time halted" state.

A physically based black hole drawn on a 2D canvas. The disk's shape comes from light bending around the hole (Beloborodov's approximation), its brightness from Doppler beaming and gravitational redshift, and background stars are lensed into arcs.

The disk is rendered once at high density; only orbiting hot spots and stars move each frame.

Click anywhere on the home page to halt time. A shockwave sweeps the starfield from the click point, the orbits lurch backwards before locking still, and the star's streak retracts into its core.

Built with the Web Animations API (orbit playback rates) and an on-demand canvas, so nothing renders while the scene is still.
